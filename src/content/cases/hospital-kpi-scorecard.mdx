## Problem

You are an analytics consultant hired by the board of trustees of "Shanti Memorial Hospital", a 120-bed multi-specialty hospital in a Tier-2 Indian city. The Medical Superintendent and Managing Director seek your guidance:
> *"Our financial margins are shrinking despite steady outpatient footfall. Department heads report conflicting numbers: nursing claims we are short-staffed, billing complains about insurance denials, and doctors say beds are frequently empty. We need an objective, executive-ready Monthly KPI Scorecard that provides transparency across bed utilization, outpatient clinic flow, clinical quality, and revenue cycle health."*

Your task is to take the hospital's raw operational data from the past 30 days, define 6 foundational hospital KPIs with exact formulas, compute the empirical scorecard, evaluate performance against clinical benchmarks, and recommend top operational priorities.

## Data you have

Monthly operational activity summary for the 30-day reporting period:
- **Total Operational Inpatient Beds**: 120 beds
- **Days in Reporting Month**: 30 days
- **Total Inpatient Days Occupied**: 1,968 patient-days
- **Total Inpatient Discharges**: 410 patients
- **Outpatient (OPD) Consultations Completed**: 6,200 visits
- **Scheduled OPD Appointments (No-Shows)**: 810 missed appointments
- **Emergency Room (ER) Acute Arrivals**: 940 arrivals
- **Clinical Laboratory Test Reports Ordered**: 5,400 reports
- **Lab Reports Delivered On-Time ($\le$ 6 Hours)**: 4,860 reports
- **Unplanned 30-Day Readmissions**: 41 patients
- **TPA / Health Insurance Claims Submitted**: 300 claims
- **Health Insurance Claims Denied / Rejected**: 36 claims

## Approach

1. **Establish the Mathematical Formulas**: Formalize exact numerators, denominators, and units for the 6 core healthcare KPIs.
2. **Compute Empirical Scores**: Calculate the monthly metrics using the verified hospital activity data.
3. **Benchmark Against Indian Healthcare Standards**: Compare observed metrics against National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers (NABH) standards.
4. **Identify Financial & Quality Leakages**: Highlight the divergence between bed occupancy, claim rejections, and readmission rates.
5. **Structure Executive Recommendations**: Provide prioritized actions for clinical and administrative leadership.

## Step-by-step solution

### Step 1: Defining the 6 Core Hospital KPIs

Here are the formal mathematical definitions:

```text
1. Bed Occupancy Rate (%) = (Total Inpatient Days / (Available Beds * Days in Month)) * 100
2. Average Length of Stay (ALOS, Days) = Total Inpatient Days / Total Discharges
3. OPD No-Show Rate (%) = (Missed Appointments / (Completed Visits + Missed Appointments)) * 100
4. On-Time Lab Turnaround Rate (%) = (Reports Delivered within 6h / Total Reports Ordered) * 100
5. 30-Day All-Cause Readmission Rate (%) = (Unplanned Readmissions within 30d / Total Discharges) * 100
6. Insurance Claim Rejection Rate (%) = (Claims Rejected by TPA / Total Claims Submitted) * 100
```

### Step 2: Calculating the Monthly Operational Scorecard

Let's compute each metric:

1. **Bed Occupancy Rate**:
   ```text
   Available Bed-Days = 120 beds * 30 days = 3,600 bed-days
   Occupancy Rate = (1,968 / 3,600) * 100 = 54.67%
   ```
2. **Average Length of Stay (ALOS)**:
   ```text
   ALOS = 1,968 patient-days / 410 discharges = 4.80 days
   ```
3. **OPD No-Show Rate**:
   ```text
   Total Booked OPD Appointments = 6,200 + 810 = 7,010 appointments
   No-Show Rate = (810 / 7,010) * 100 = 11.55% (~11.6%)
   ```
4. **On-Time Diagnostic Lab Turnaround Rate**:
   ```text
   On-Time Lab Rate = (4,860 / 5,400) * 100 = 90.00%
   ```
5. **30-Day Unplanned Readmission Rate**:
   ```text
   Readmission Rate = (41 / 410) * 100 = 10.00%
   ```
6. **Insurance Claim Rejection Rate**:
   ```text
   Claim Rejection Rate = (36 / 300) * 100 = 12.00%
   ```

### Step 3: The Complete Executive KPI Scorecard

| KPI Name | Formula | Hospital Value | NABH / Industry Benchmark | Status (RAG) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bed Occupancy Rate** | Inpatient Days / (Beds $\times$ 30) | **54.67%** | 75.0% – 85.0% | **Red (Under-utilized)** |
| **Average Length of Stay (ALOS)** | Inpatient Days / Discharges | **4.80 days** | 3.5 – 4.5 days | **Yellow (Slightly high)** |
| **OPD No-Show Rate** | Missed / Total Scheduled | **11.55%** | $\le$ 5.0% | **Red (Lost clinic revenue)** |
| **On-Time Lab Turnaround** | Reports $\le$ 6h / Total Reports | **90.00%** | $\ge$ 95.0% | **Yellow (Near target)** |
| **30-Day Readmission Rate** | Readmissions / Discharges | **10.00%** | $\le$ 5.0% | **Red (Clinical concern)** |
| **Claim Rejection Rate** | Denied Claims / Filed Claims | **12.00%** | $\le$ 3.0% – 4.0% | **Red (Cash-flow bleed)** |

### Step 4: Diagnosing the Hospital's Performance

1. **Sub-Optimal Bed Utilization**: At 54.67% occupancy, nearly 55 of the hospital's 120 beds sit empty every night, wasting fixed nursing and infrastructure overhead.
2. **OPD Clinic Capacity Leakage**: 810 missed appointments represent roughly 27 idle doctor consultation slots every single day, forfeiting pharmacy and downstream diagnostic revenue.
3. **Severe Financial Bleed from Claim Rejections**: A 12.00% TPA claim denial rate (36 rejected claims) delays working capital and forces bad-debt write-offs on high-value surgical cases.
4. **Clinical Quality Alarm**: A 10.00% 30-day readmission rate indicates premature discharges or inadequate post-discharge medication adherence counseling.

## Key insight

Shanti Memorial Hospital does not suffer from a lack of community demand; it suffers from operational and clinical friction. Low bed occupancy (54.67%) is exacerbated by an 11.6% outpatient no-show rate, while bottom-line profitability is eroded by a 12.0% insurance denial rate and a 10.0% readmission rate. Addressing administrative friction in outpatient reminders, discharge planning, and claims documentation will transform operational margins without adding new beds.

## Recommendation

1. **Automated WhatsApp OPD Reminders and Overbooking**:
   - Deploy automated two-way WhatsApp appointment reminders 24 hours and 2 hours prior to scheduled OPD slots. Implement a controlled 10% overbooking model for high no-show specialties (e.g. general medicine) to achieve full doctor utilization.
2. **Pre-Submission Insurance Claim Scrubbers**:
   - Claim rejections at 12% are unsustainable. Implement an administrative checklist ensuring that pre-authorization codes, doctor notes, and diagnostic bills match TPA coding guidelines before claim submission, cutting denials below 4%.
3. **Post-Discharge 48-Hour Nursing Follow-Up Calls**:
   - Establish a dedicated telephonic follow-up protocol where a nurse contacts all discharged surgical and medical patients within 48 hours to confirm medication compliance, spot early complications, and reduce readmissions from 10% down to the 5% benchmark.

## What a weak answer looks like

- Recommends building an expensive new hospital wing when existing beds are already 45% empty.
- Calculates Bed Occupancy by dividing discharges by beds (410 / 120 = 341%), confusing flow with stock.
- Calculates OPD no-show rate as `810 / 6,200` instead of using total scheduled appointments as the denominator.
- Fails to connect claim denials and no-shows to bottom-line financial sustainability.
