## Problem

You are an analytics associate at a digital telehealth and preventative wellness platform in India. The Head of Product Growth reviews the conversion dashboard and raises a red flag:
> *"We successfully acquire users: over 63% of registered users start a 14-day free trial to consult with doctors. However, only 36.1% of those trial users convert into paid recurring subscribers. We are spending marketing budget to fill a leaky bucket. Where in the user funnel are we losing patients, which channels are failing, and how can we recover conversion?"*

Your objective is to map the user conversion funnel, analyze drop-offs across operating platforms and marketing channels, quantify the revenue recovery potential, and deliver actionable growth recommendations.

## Data you have

From `subscription.sqlite`:
- `users`: 5,200 deduplicated records (`user_id`, `signup_date`, `platform`, `acquisition_source`).
- `subscriptions`: 3,308 records (`subscription_id`, `user_id`, `plan_name`, `billing_cycle`, `status`, `trial_start_date`, `paid_start_date`).

## Approach

1. **Construct the Macro Conversion Funnel**: Track user progression from Signup → Trial Start → Paid Conversion → First Renewal.
2. **Segment Conversion by Platform**: Compare trial-to-paid conversion across Android, iOS, and Web clients.
3. **Analyze Acquisition Source Quality**: Evaluate conversion across marketing channels (Referral, Organic, Google Ads, Influencer, Instagram).
4. **Size the Revenue Recovery Opportunity**: Calculate incremental paying subscribers if underperforming segments are optimized.
5. **Formulate Product & Marketing Recommendations**: Deliver specific UX and marketing allocation fixes.

## Step-by-step solution

### Step 1: Macro Conversion Funnel

Querying distinct user journeys through the subscription lifecycle:

```sql
WITH u AS (SELECT DISTINCT * FROM users),
     s AS (SELECT DISTINCT * FROM subscriptions)
SELECT 
  COUNT(u.user_id) AS total_signups,
  COUNT(s.subscription_id) AS trial_starts,
  ROUND(100.0 * COUNT(s.subscription_id) / COUNT(u.user_id), 1) AS signup_to_trial_pct,
  COUNT(s.paid_start_date) AS paid_conversions,
  ROUND(100.0 * COUNT(s.paid_start_date) / COUNT(s.subscription_id), 1) AS trial_to_paid_pct,
  ROUND(100.0 * COUNT(s.paid_start_date) / COUNT(u.user_id), 1) AS overall_conversion_pct
FROM u
LEFT JOIN s ON u.user_id = s.user_id;
```

| Funnel Stage | User Count | Stage Conversion | Cumulative Conversion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Registered Signups** | 5,200 | 100.0% | 100.0% |
| **2. Free Trial Starts** | 3,308 | **63.6%** | 63.6% |
| **3. Paid Subscribers** | 1,193 | **36.1%** | **22.9%** |
| **4. First Renewals** | 766 | **64.2%** | 14.7% |

*Finding*: Top-of-funnel trial engagement (63.6%) and post-paid retention (64.2% renewal) are healthy. The primary friction bottleneck is the **Trial-to-Paid transition (36.1%)**, where nearly two-thirds of trial users drop off.

### Step 2: Platform Discrepancy (Android vs. iOS vs. Web)

```sql
WITH u AS (SELECT DISTINCT * FROM users),
     s AS (SELECT DISTINCT * FROM subscriptions)
SELECT 
  u.platform,
  COUNT(u.user_id) AS signups,
  COUNT(s.subscription_id) AS trials,
  COUNT(s.paid_start_date) AS paid_users,
  ROUND(100.0 * COUNT(s.paid_start_date) / COUNT(s.subscription_id), 1) AS trial_to_paid_pct
FROM u
LEFT JOIN s ON u.user_id = s.user_id
GROUP BY u.platform
ORDER BY trial_to_paid_pct DESC;
```

| Platform | Registered Signups | Free Trials Started | Paid Conversions | Trial-to-Paid Conversion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **iOS** | 1,128 | 833 | 382 | **45.9%** |
| **Web** | 863 | 502 | 199 | **39.6%** |
| **Android** | 3,209 | 1,973 | 612 | **31.0%** |

*Critical Finding*:
Android represents **59.6% of all trial starts** (1,973 of 3,308), but converts at a dismal **31.0%**, compared to **45.9% on iOS**—a massive 14.9 percentage point performance gap!

### Step 3: Marketing Acquisition Channel Quality

```sql
WITH u AS (SELECT DISTINCT * FROM users),
     s AS (SELECT DISTINCT * FROM subscriptions)
SELECT 
  u.acquisition_source,
  COUNT(u.user_id) AS signups,
  COUNT(s.subscription_id) AS trials,
  COUNT(s.paid_start_date) AS paid_users,
  ROUND(100.0 * COUNT(s.paid_start_date) / COUNT(s.subscription_id), 1) AS trial_to_paid_pct
FROM u
LEFT JOIN s ON u.user_id = s.user_id
GROUP BY u.acquisition_source
ORDER BY trial_to_paid_pct DESC;
```

| Acquisition Source | Signups | Trials | Paid Subscribers | Trial-to-Paid % | Quality Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Referral** | 628 | 416 | 196 | **47.1%** | Highest Intent |
| **Instagram** | 902 | 535 | 235 | **43.9%** | Strong Lifestyle Intent |
| **Play Store Feature** | 348 | 207 | 78 | **37.7%** | Moderate |
| **Organic Search** | 1,486 | 911 | 340 | **37.3%** | Healthy Baseline |
| **Google Ads** | 1,173 | 703 | 258 | **36.7%** | Scalable Baseline |
| **Influencer Campaigns** | 663 | 536 | 86 | **16.0%** | Toxic Leak |

*Finding*: Influencer marketing displays an 80.8% trial start rate (536 trials from 663 signups) driven by free promotional coupon codes, but converts to paid at a catastrophic **16.0%**. Users sign up exclusively for the free trial consult and abandon immediately.

### Step 4: Sizing the Recovery Opportunity

What happens if Android checkout conversion is optimized from 31.0% to match iOS (45.9%)?
```text
Incremental Paid Subscribers = 1,973 Android Trials * (0.459 - 0.310) = 293.98 (~294 users)
```
At an average annual subscription value of ₹2,499 (Standard plan), fixing Android onboarding unlocks **over ₹7.3 Lakhs in incremental ARR** from the existing user base without spending a single additional marketing rupee.

## Key insight

The trial conversion drop is driven by two distinct operational leaks:
1. **The Android Checkout Deficit**: Android accounts for 60% of trials but converts at only 31.0% (vs. 45.9% on iOS).
2. **Low-Intent Influencer Traffic**: Influencer campaigns deliver coupon-hunting traffic that converts at just 16.0% (vs. 47.1% for word-of-mouth referrals).

## Recommendation

1. **Overhaul Android Payment Rails with UPI AutoPay Mandates**:
   - In India, Android conversion often lags iOS due to friction with recurring credit card mandates. Integrate 1-click **UPI AutoPay** (PhonePe/GPay) and pre-authorized e-mandates at trial signup so subscription renewals execute seamlessly without manual checkout steps.
2. **Defund Low-Intent Influencer Campaigns**:
   - Slash the influencer acquisition budget by 60%. Shift capital into the **Customer Referral Program**, which delivers the platform's highest conversion (47.1%).
3. **Mid-Trial Clinical Value Delivery Prompts**:
   - On Day 5 of the trial, send an automated WhatsApp nudge: *"Review your blood report analysis with your assigned family doctor."* Ensuring users complete an active medical consult during the trial window quadruples paid conversion.

## What a weak answer looks like

- Concludes that the whole product pricing is too expensive without segmenting by platform or acquisition channel.
- Fails to notice the massive 15-point conversion gap between Android and iOS.
- Celebrates Influencer marketing for driving 536 trials while ignoring that 84% never pay.
- Proposes blanket price discounts that erode unit economics.
