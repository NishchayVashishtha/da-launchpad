- title "Last 7 days and last 24 hours before the interview" | description "A day-by-day plan and a calm checklist for the final hours."
- objectives: Follow a 7-day revision plan; Prepare logistics and documents; Rehearse project pitch and HR answers; Run a final 24-hour checklist; Manage nerves
- tags ["checklist","revision","interview-day"]
## Concept
- Analogy: cricket team's pre-match routine: no new tactics, only polish.
- 7-day plan table (Day -7 .. -1): D7 map the JD + company research; D6 SQL patterns (joins, window, group) 90 min + 20 timed questions; D5 stats/Excel/Power BI essentials + 15 MCQs; D4 project pitch (2-min script) + 10 project questions; D3 HR answers (tell me about yourself, why DA, why fintech, UI/UX switch story) aloud; D2 full mock interview (use site mock mode) + case; D1 light revision + logistics. Tailor hours to 5-6 h/day.
- 24-hour checklist: sleep 7 h; documents (resume x3, ID, marksheets, offer letters); internet/power backup; laptop charged, Zoom/Meet test, camera, quiet room, tabs closed; notepad; water; dress; reach 10 min early; company one-liner + 3 questions to ask; review own resume line by line; cheat sheets (SQL, stats formulas) skim only.
- Last 60 minutes: breathe, 3 talking points, warm-up SQL query.
- During interview: pause before answering, structure answers, ask for clarification, STAR for stories.
- After: thank-you email within 24 h (template), note questions asked, update tracker.
- If nervous: box breathing 4-4-4-4 steps.
- Personalise: switch story from UI/UX to DA in 3 sentences; mention fintech interest; relocation flexible.
- Provide two checklists as markdown task lists; also a `<Download>` NOT needed.
## Worked examples (3): 1 Sample 7-day calendar for Nishchay-like learner (table). 2 Warm-up SQL on fintech (SqlPlayground: `SELECT payment_app, COUNT(*) FROM transactions GROUP BY payment_app ORDER BY 2 DESC;` real output) as 10-minute warm-up. 3 Thank-you email example (80 words).
## Common mistakes: cramming new topics last night; no tech test; arriving late; no questions to ask; badmouthing UI/UX internship or current employer.
## Quiz 5: best use of last night; what to bring; first thing in the call; thank-you timing; HR trap question.
## Interview Qs 3: Why are you switching from UI/UX? How did you prepare for this interview? What questions do you have for us?
