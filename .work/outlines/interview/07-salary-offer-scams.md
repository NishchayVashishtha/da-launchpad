## Concept (India fresher context; all numbers illustrative; say "roughly, varies by company and regime")
- Fresher DA pay bands (say "typical ranges seen in 2025-26 job posts; check current postings"): services Rs 3.5-6 LPA; product/fintech startups Rs 5-10 LPA; GCC/consulting Rs 8-14 LPA. Mark as approximate, advise checking live postings; no company names.
- CTC components: fixed (basic ~40-50%, HRA, special allowance), employer PF (12% of basic), gratuity (after 5 yrs), insurance, variable pay, joining/retention bonus, ESOPs, relocation. In-hand = CTC minus employer PF, gratuity, insurance, variable not guaranteed, minus employee PF, professional tax, income tax (TDS).
- FormulaCard: In-hand per month ~ (Fixed gross - employee PF - PT - TDS)/12. Show worked CTC Rs 6,00,000 -> fixed Rs 5,40,000 + variable Rs 60,000 -> basic 50% of fixed = Rs 2,70,000 -> employee PF 12% of basic Rs 32,400/yr -> approx monthly in-hand before tax ~ Rs 42,300 (compute; tax rules vary, state new regime rebate may bring TDS to ~0 at this level, tell learner to verify).
- Bonds/service agreements: training bond 1-2 yrs, penalty Rs 50k-2L; check enforceability generally, read clauses on notice period, clawback of joining bonus, non-compete; ask HR in writing. Not legal advice.
- Variable pay: guaranteed vs performance-linked; ask payout history.
- Evaluating offers: compare in-hand, learning, growth, manager, WFH, bond, tech stack, brand, location cost (rent in metro vs tier-2) - scoring table template.
- Negotiation for freshers: when (after written offer), how: research, anchor with range, use value + competing offer, ask non-salary (joining bonus, review at 6 months, learning budget, WFH days). 3 scripts: email counter, phone "expected salary" deflect, accept-with-ask.
- Expected salary answer at HR round: give range based on research, "flexible for the right role".
- Offer letter checklist (12 items) and background-verification honesty.
- Red flags: asking payment (training/registration/laptop deposit), WhatsApp/Telegram-only hiring, free email domain (gmail), no interview, unrealistic pay for fresher, "work from home typing/data entry", demands Aadhaar/OTP/bank details early, offers before interview, urgency pressure. What to do: verify domain, LinkedIn, MCA/company check, report on cybercrime.gov.in/1930 (fine as plain text), never pay.
- Personalise: graduating 2027, off-campus and fintech-first; remote ok: ask about location-based pay; no bond preferred, stay flexible since goal is DE/AE in 2-3 years: choose learning-heavy role over +Rs 50k.

## Common mistakes: quoting CTC as take-home; ignoring bond; accepting verbal offers; negotiating before written offer; paying "registration fees"; lying about current salary (fresher: say none/stipend honestly).
