## Concept
- Analogy: OA is a sprint; accuracy x speed; 60-90 sec per question budget.
- Sections, each with a FormulaCard and 2 shortcut tricks:
 1 Percentages: fraction table 1/2=50, 1/3=33.33, 1/4=25, 1/5=20, 1/6=16.67, 1/7=14.28, 1/8=12.5, 1/9=11.11, 1/11=9.09; x% of y = y% of x; successive change a+b+ab/100; 
 2 Ratio and proportion: unit method, componendo-light, ratio of ratios chain A:B and B:C make B equal.
 3 Averages: assumed mean; change in average x number of items; weighted average via alligation (mixture cross method).
 4 Profit and loss: CP=100 trick; markup/discount: net = a - b - ab/100; SP=CP(1+p%).
 5 Time and work: total work = LCM; pipes as negative work; efficiency ratio inverse of days; A,B together = ab/(a+b).
 6 Time-speed-distance: avg speed same distance = 2xy/(x+y); relative speed; km/h to m/s x5/18.
 7 Probability/permutations: P(at least one)=1-P(none); nCr symmetry; arrangements with repeats; circular (n-1)!.
 8 Logical reasoning: tables for seating/ranking; draw it; eliminate; syllogism Venn rule.
 9 Data interpretation: read units/axes first, estimate (round to 2 digits), percentage change vs percentage points, caselets -> make a table; pie chart angle = %x3.6.
 10 Verbal basics: subject-verb, one-line elimination rules.
 11 Test strategy: first pass easy, mark and skip >2 min, no negative marking = guess, 3 passes; calculator absent -> estimate options.
- Use Indian contexts (GST 18%, UPI cashback, lakh, train/bus).
