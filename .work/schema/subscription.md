## subscription

- **users** (5208 rows): user_id:INTEGER, email:TEXT, signup_date:DATE, signup_time:TEXT, city:TEXT, platform:TEXT, acquisition_source:TEXT, age_group:TEXT
- **subscriptions** (3308 rows): subscription_id:INTEGER, user_id:INTEGER, plan:TEXT, billing_cycle:TEXT, price_inr:INTEGER, trial_start_date:DATE, paid_start_date:DATE, end_date:DATE, cancel_date:DATE, status:TEXT, renewal_count:INTEGER
- **events** (36383 rows): event_id:INTEGER, user_id:INTEGER, event_time:DATETIME, event_name:TEXT, plan:TEXT, platform:TEXT
- FKs: subscriptions.user_id->users.user_id; events.user_id->users.user_id
- Quality issues: events: ~0.5% (about 100) exact duplicate rows (same event_id); users: 8 exact duplicates. Neither table has a PRIMARY KEY constraint on its id. | events.event_name: ~1.2% of values are UPPERCASE ('APP_OPEN'); events.platform: ~2% are lowercase ('android') - breaks naive GROUP BY. | users.email ~6% NULL; users.city ~4% NULL and ~10% inconsistent casing/whitespace. | events.plan is NULL for events before a plan is chosen (signup and most early engagement events); subscriptions.paid_start_date NULL for users who never paid; cancel_date/end_date NULL by design for active users. | CSV only - users.signup_date ~15% and subscriptions.trial_start_date ~10%: DD-MM-YYYY; events.event_time ~10%: DD-MM-YYYY HH:MM:SS. | CSV only - subscriptions.price_inr: ~40% of rows are text such as '₹299'.
