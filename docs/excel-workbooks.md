# Excel practice workbooks

Generated by `scripts/generate_data/excel_workbooks.py`. Do not edit by hand; rerun the script instead.

Files are in `public/data/excel/`. Every workbook starts with a `README` sheet that lists the other sheets. Row counts below exclude the header row. Column names are exact and match the header cells.

Sample data comes from the CSVs in `public/data/csv/` with `random_state=42`. A blank cell means the value was missing in the source CSV or does not apply to that row.

## excel-cleaning.xlsx

Path: `public/data/excel/excel-cleaning.xlsx`

Two sheets of deliberately messy data for practising TRIM, CLEAN, Text to Columns, Remove Duplicates, Find & Replace and Flash Fill. The data is synthetic and is based on the ecommerce customers and products tables.

| Sheet | Data rows | Excel Table | Columns (in order) |
|---|---|---|---|
| README | (index) | no | Sheet, What it contains, Data rows |
| `customers_messy` | 1,272 | no | `customer_id`, `full_name`, `email`, `phone`, `gender`, `city`, `state`, `signup_date`, `acquisition_channel`, `is_prime` |
| `products_messy` | 420 | no | `product_id`, `sku`, `product_name`, `category`, `sub_category`, `brand`, `mrp`, `cost_price`, `is_active` |

Sheet descriptions:

- `customers_messy`: 1,272 customer rows (1,200 unique customers plus 72 exact duplicate rows) with messy data: extra and missing spaces, mixed casing in city, email and full_name, phone numbers in six formats, and signup_date stored as text in five date formats. Columns: customer_id, full_name, email, phone, gender, city, state, signup_date, acquisition_channel, is_prime.
- `products_messy`: 420 product rows (every product in the catalogue) where product_name contains junk characters such as leading and trailing asterisks, non-breaking spaces, zero-width spaces, tabs, the trademark sign and trailing ' ##' markers. Columns: product_id, sku, product_name, category, sub_category, brand, mrp, cost_price, is_active.

Notes:

- customers_messy: signup_date is text, not a real date. Slash dates are day/month/year (DD/MM/YYYY).
- customers_messy: 72 rows are exact copies of other rows (same customer_id). Use Remove Duplicates on customer_id.
- customers_messy: phone is text. Blank phone cells are genuinely missing.
- products_messy: product_name is the only column with junk characters. The other columns are clean numbers and codes.

## excel-functions.xlsx

Path: `public/data/excel/excel-functions.xlsx`

Ecommerce, HR and target data for practising IF/IFS/AND/OR, VLOOKUP, HLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, INDEX-MATCH, SUMIFS/COUNTIFS/AVERAGEIFS, text and date functions, and FILTER/UNIQUE/SORT. Sheets are joined by ID (order.customer_id to customers.customer_id, order.main_product_id to products.product_id).

| Sheet | Data rows | Excel Table | Columns (in order) |
|---|---|---|---|

| `products` | 420 | tbl_products | `product_id`, `sku`, `product_name`, `category`, `sub_category`, `brand`, `mrp`, `cost_price`, `is_active` |
| `hr_employees` | 2,400 | tbl_hr_employees | `employee_id`, `full_name`, `gender`, `date_of_birth`, `hire_date`, `department_id`, `department_name`, `job_title`, `job_level`, `manager_id`, `city`, `employment_type`, `work_mode`, `employment_status` |
| `monthly_targets` | 2 | no | `Metric`, `Jan-2023 ... Dec-2023 (columns B to M)` |

Sheet descriptions:

- `orders`: 2,000 ecommerce orders (random sample, sorted by order_id). One row per order. Columns: order_id, customer_id, order_date, order_time, order_status, sales_channel, coupon_code, discount_amount, shipping_fee, total_amount, delivery_date, items_count (number of order lines), main_product_id (product on the order's highest-value line).
- `customers`: 1,063 customers who placed at least one order in the orders sheet, with cleaned city casing and real dates. Columns: customer_id, full_name, city, state, gender, signup_date, acquisition_channel, is_prime (1 = Prime member, 0 = not).
- `products`: 420 products from the catalogue (all of them), with cleaned category casing. Columns: product_id, sku, product_name, category, sub_category, brand, mrp, cost_price, is_active (1 = active).
- `hr_employees`: 2,400 employees (one row per employee_id) with the department name looked up from the departments table. Columns: employee_id, full_name, gender, date_of_birth, hire_date, department_id, department_name, job_title, job_level, manager_id, city, employment_type, work_mode, employment_status.
- `monthly_targets`: Small horizontal table for HLOOKUP practice. Row 1 holds the 12 month start dates of 2023 (Jan-2023 to Dec-2023) across columns B to M. Row 2 is the target order count per month and row 3 is the target revenue in INR per month. Column A holds the row labels.

Notes:

- orders: order_date is a real date. The source mixed YYYY-MM-DD and DD-MM-YYYY text, which was converted.
- orders: coupon_code and delivery_date are blank where the order had no coupon or was not delivered.
- customers: only customers who appear in the orders sheet are included, so every customer_id in orders can be looked up.
- hr_employees: department_name comes from the department lookup. Use it to practise INDEX-MATCH or XLOOKUP.
- monthly_targets: targets were set as 2023 actuals multiplied by a random factor between 0.95 and 1.15. Actuals come from the full ecommerce order table, so the targets do not match the 2,000-row orders sheet.

## excel-hr-attrition.xlsx

Path: `public/data/excel/excel-hr-attrition.xlsx`

One row per employee with department, tenure, latest review, latest salary and an attrition flag. Built for attrition analysis and dashboard tasks.

| Sheet | Data rows | Excel Table | Columns (in order) |
|---|---|---|---|
| README | (index) | no | Sheet, What it contains, Data rows |
| `employees` | 2,400 | tbl_employees | `employee_id`, `gender`, `age`, `hire_date`, `tenure_years`, `department_name`, `job_title`, `job_level`, `city`, `employment_type`, `work_mode`, `employment_status`, `latest_review_period`, `latest_manager_rating`, `latest_annual_ctc_inr`, `latest_salary_effective_date`, `attrition_flag`, `exit_date`, `exit_type`, `exit_reason`, `regretted_loss`, `exit_interview_score` |

Sheet descriptions:

- `employees`: 2,400 employees (one row per employee_id). attrition_flag is 1 when the employee has a row in the attrition table (exit_date, exit_type, exit_reason, regretted_loss, exit_interview_score are filled only for leavers). latest_review_period and latest_manager_rating come from the most recent performance review. latest_annual_ctc_inr and latest_salary_effective_date come from the most recent salary row. Columns: employee_id, gender, age (at 2024-12-31), hire_date, tenure_years (at 2024-12-31), department_name, job_title, job_level, city, employment_type, work_mode, employment_status, latest_review_period, latest_manager_rating, latest_annual_ctc_inr, latest_salary_effective_date, attrition_flag, exit_date, exit_type, exit_reason, regretted_loss, exit_interview_score.

Notes:

- age and tenure_years are calculated as of 2024-12-31, the end of the data period, not today.
- The source employee list had 12 employee IDs listed twice (same person, dates in a different format). Only the first copy of each ID is kept.
- employment_status and attrition_flag do not always agree in the source data. attrition_flag comes from the attrition table.
- regretted_loss is 1 for a regretted leaver and 0 for a non-regretted leaver. Blank for employees who stayed.

## excel-whatif.xlsx

Path: `public/data/excel/excel-whatif.xlsx`

Two models with live formulas for Goal Seek, Data Tables and Scenario Manager. Change the blue input cells and the rest of the sheet recalculates.

| Sheet | Data rows | Excel Table | Columns (in order) |
|---|---|---|---|
| README | (index) | no | Sheet, What it contains, Data rows |
| `loan_emi` | 36 | no | `Month`, `Opening balance`, `EMI`, `Interest`, `Principal`, `Closing balance` |
| `pricing_model` | 8 | no | `Product`, `Units sold`, `Base price (INR)`, `Base unit cost (INR)`, `Adjusted price (INR)`, `Adjusted unit cost (INR)`, `Revenue (INR)`, `Variable cost (INR)`, `Gross profit (INR)`, `Margin %` |

Sheet descriptions:

- `loan_emi`: EMI calculator. Inputs are in B3 (loan amount, Rs 5,00,000), B4 (annual rate, 10.5%) and B5 (tenure, 36 months). B7 to B10 are formulas for the monthly rate, monthly EMI, total payable and total interest. Rows 13 to 48 hold a 36-month amortisation schedule built with formulas (columns A to F).
- `pricing_model`: Pricing model for 8 active catalogue products. Units sold (column B) are typed inputs and base price and cost come from the catalogue MRP and cost price. Global inputs: B3 price change %, B4 unit cost change %, B5 fixed costs. Columns E to J and the totals in row 16 are formulas. B18 is net profit after fixed costs.

Notes:

- Formulas are not pre-calculated. Excel calculates them when the file opens.
- loan_emi: the schedule has 36 rows. If you change the tenure to a value above 36, copy the last row down.
- pricing_model: change B3 and B4 to test price and cost scenarios, or use Goal Seek on B18 by changing B3.
